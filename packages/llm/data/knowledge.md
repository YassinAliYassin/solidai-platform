# Solid Solutions — Knowledge Base

This file holds factual, first-party information about Solid Solutions and its
products. It is the single source of truth reused in two places:

1. Training corpus for the from-scratch Solid LLM (`data/build_corpus.py`).
2. Grounding context for the website assistant (`/api/chat`).

Keep statements true and current. Do not add aspirational claims here — marketing
vision copy lives on the website, not in this grounding file.

## About

Solid Solutions is a technology initiative focused on Africa's digital future.
It works on experimental AI and infrastructure projects, contributing to
emerging technical initiatives and early-stage technology development.
The organisation can be reached at info@solidsolutions.africa.
Its website is solidsolutions.africa.

## SolidAI

SolidAI is the applied-AI program of Solid Solutions. Its goal is to build AI
that is practical for the African context: understanding local languages and
powering digital infrastructure rather than staying theoretical. SolidAI focuses
on hyper-local natural language processing for diverse African dialects, edge
efficiency for low-power hardware, and a privacy-first approach that keeps
sensitive data within defined boundaries. SolidAI is currently in beta; a beta
program lets developers request early access to the APIs and models.

## Solid LLM

Solid LLM is Solid Solutions' language-model research effort. It includes a
transformer language model built from scratch in PyTorch. The current public
research preview is a small character-level model trained on first-party text;
it is an honest demonstration of the training and inference pipeline, not a
large production model. The research direction targets hyper-local NLP for
African dialects, agri-tech intelligence such as crop-disease detection,
predictive models for inclusive financial services, and edge deployment on
low-power hardware.

## Sectors SolidAI advises on

- Agriculture: crop selection, soil management, pest control, irrigation, and
  sustainable farming for African climates and small-scale farmers.
- Health: preventive care, malaria, HIV/AIDS and TB, maternal and child health,
  nutrition, and healthcare access in resource-limited settings.
- Education: curriculum development, teacher training, digital literacy, and
  reaching remote or underserved students.
- Finance: mobile money, microfinance, savings groups, digital payments, and
  financial inclusion for unbanked and underbanked populations.
- Legal: land rights, business registration, contract basics, and access to
  justice through community-based mechanisms.
- Transport: last-mile delivery, public transit, rural road access, and
  cross-border logistics.
- Energy: off-grid solar, mini-grids, clean cooking, and pay-as-you-go energy
  access for rural areas.

## Privacy

Solid Solutions collects only basic contact information that visitors submit
voluntarily, uses it solely to respond to inquiries and share updates, and does
not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing.
